PRIVACY
POLICY

Last updated: January 2025  ·  Next Rides Uganda

1. Introduction

Next Rides Uganda ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our clients, website visitors, and anyone who contacts us. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Identity data: full name, date of birth, national ID or passport number
  • Contact data: phone number, email address, physical address
  • Transaction data: details of vehicles purchased, rented, or ordered
  • Financial data: payment method details (we do not store card numbers)
  • Communication data: messages sent to us via WhatsApp, email or our website
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, and pages visited on our website
  • Vehicle data: driving licence details required for rentals

3. How We Collect Information

We collect information when you contact us via WhatsApp, email, or phone; when you complete a purchase, rental agreement, or order form; when you visit our website; and when we are required to collect it for legal or regulatory compliance purposes such as URA customs declarations.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Process vehicle sales, rentals, and import orders
  • Communicate with you about your enquiry or transaction
  • Comply with URA, UNRA, and other legal obligations
  • Improve our services and website experience
  • Send you information about new arrivals or promotions (only if you have opted in)

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with:

  • URA (Uganda Revenue Authority) for customs and duty processing
  • UNRA (Uganda National Roads Authority) for vehicle registration
  • Shipping agents and freight forwarders for import logistics
  • Banks and mobile money processors to process payments
  • Professional advisors (legal, accounting) where necessary

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Communication via WhatsApp is end-to-end encrypted. We do not store payment card numbers on our systems.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, and for as long as required by Ugandan law. Transaction records are typically retained for seven years for tax and legal purposes.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, request deletion of your information where we have no legal obligation to retain it, and withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at info.nextridesug@gmail.com.

9. Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies to improve your browsing experience. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting your use of our site.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to social media platforms (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Threads) and Pinterest. These platforms have their own privacy policies which we encourage you to review. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The most current version will always be available on this page with the date of last update shown above.
